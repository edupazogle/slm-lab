# E1 Dataset Information

## Source
- **OpenPII 1.5M**: ai4privacy/pii-masking-openpii-1.5m (CC-BY-4.0)
  - URL: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4privacy/pii-masking-openpii-1.5m
  - License: CC-BY-4.0 (commercial use permitted with attribution)
  - Attribution: Ai4Privacy / Ai Suisse SA
  - Description: Multilingual PII Masking Dataset with 30 languages including French and English

## Composition
- 400 French rows sampled from OpenPII 1.5M
- 400 English rows sampled from OpenPII 1.5M  
- 100 synthetic French claim emails generated by gen_fr_claims.py

## Entity Types Mapped
From OpenPII to our types:
- EMAIL → EMAIL
- GIVENNAME, SURNAME → PERSON
- TELEPHONENUM → PHONE
- DATE → DATE
- STREET + BUILDINGNUM → ADDRESS
- ZIPCODE → POSTCODE
- CITY → CITY
- SOCIALNUM → NATIONAL_ID
- TAXNUM → TAX_ID
- IDCARDNUM, PASSPORTNUM, DRIVERLICENSENUM → ID_DOC
- CREDITCARDNUMBER → CARD
- TITLE, AGE, GENDER, SEX → recorded but out of scoring

## Direct Identifiers for Leak Rate
PERSON, EMAIL, PHONE, NATIONAL_ID/NIR, IBAN, CARD, PLATE, ID_DOC, ADDRESS

## Counts
- Total rows: 900 (800 from OpenPII + 100 synthetic)
- French: 500 (400 OpenPII + 100 synthetic)
- English: 400 (all from OpenPII)
